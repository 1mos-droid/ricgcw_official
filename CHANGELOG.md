# Changelog

All notable changes to the ICGCW Official Website will be documented in this file.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-04-09

### Added
- Comprehensive Church Identity section including Scriptural Foundation (Esther 5:1).
- Motto, Slogan, and Vision statements.
- Biblical Context and Definitions (Inner, Court, Gospel, Church).
- Temple of Solomon historical context and related scriptures.
- Symbolic Colors update with full spiritual meanings.
- Ecclesiastical Authority section.

### Changed
- Rebranded from "Rhema Inner Court Gospel Church" (RICGCW) to "INNER COURT GOSPEL CHURCH (WORLDWIDE)" (ICGCW).
- Updated all meta tags, titles, and email addresses to reflect new branding.
- Fixed Gold color symbolic meaning.
- Added Black to official church colors.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-04-08

### Added
- **Initial Launch**: Complete high-fidelity official website.
- **iOS Design System**: Implemented 24px squircle radii, glassmorphism, and premium typography.
- **Hero Section**: Captivating vision statement with dynamic motion background.
- **About Section**: Detailed history and leadership profile for Overseer Rev. Nicholas Dobeng.
- **Ministries Grid**: Interactive 6-block community overview with unique iconography.
- **Media Hub**: Integrated sampler for Radio and YouTube spiritual broadcasts.
- **Inspiration Widget**: Dynamic "Verse of the Day" component with social share capabilities.
- **Calendar of Glory**: Visual event timeline for upcoming services and programs.
- **Connect Portal**: Comprehensive contact hub with interactive form and social links.
- **Sticky Navbar**: Responsive, glassmorphic navigation with mobile drawer.

### Technical
- Scaffolded with React 19, Vite 8, and TypeScript.
- Configured Tailwind CSS 4 for design system enforcement.
- Integrated Framer Motion for high-end reveal and hover animations.
- Implemented responsive breakpoints for consistent experience across devices.
